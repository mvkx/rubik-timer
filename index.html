Saved Times

    00:00.000

    Keybinds: Space = Start/Stop, S = Save, R = DNF

    The main button cycles Start, Stop, Save. DNF logs a running or unsaved solve as a DNF. Start again after Save or DNF.

    Inspection (optional): the first press starts a 15s countdown. Starting at 15–17s adds +2, past 17s is a DNF. R or Cancel aborts inspection.

    +2 buttons (optional): hover a saved time and click +2 to toggle the penalty.

    Splits (optional): pick a number of phases, or a CFOP/Roux preset, and name them. While solving, Space (or the Split button) marks the end of a phase; the last press stops the timer. Use the ▾ button on a saved time to see its phases (gold = best so far, ± = vs. your average). The 📊 button shows per-phase stats.

    All saved times stay in your browser via local storage.
